Privacy Policy - Zman

Last updated: September 17, 2026
This policy is published at: https://zimna-eta.vercel.app/privacy.html

Zman - Halachic Times & Alarms ("Zman", "the App", "we", "us") is an alarm clock and reference app for Jewish daily halachic times (zmanim). This policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, and your choices.

Summary

  • Zman does not require an account and does not collect or store any personal data on our servers.
  • Your location and alarm settings are stored only on your own device.
  • We do not sell data, run ads, or use tracking/analytics tools.

Location Data

If you grant location permission, the App uses your device's GPS/network location to calculate accurate zmanim (halachic times) for your area, using standard astronomical calculations performed entirely on your device. Your location is saved only in your device's local browser storage so the App can remember it between sessions. It is not transmitted to us or to any third-party server. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; you can also set your location manually by selecting a city in the App.

Alarms, Notifications & Preferences

Alarm times, snooze state, display preferences (language, view mode, which times are shown), and onboarding status are all stored locally on your device (via browser local storage) so the App remembers your settings. This data stays on your device and is deleted if you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. Alarm notifications are generated and delivered locally by your device; we do not operate a push-notification server and do not see when your alarms fire.

AI "Fun Fact" Feature

The App includes an optional feature that shares a short, kid-friendly fun fact about a zman (e.g. "Shkia" or "Chatzos"). To generate this text, the name of the topic is sent to Google's Gemini API. No location data, alarm data, or any other personal information is included in this request. Use of this feature is subject to Google's Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App loads a small number of external resources needed to display correctly:

  • Google Fonts - for the app's typography. Your device requests fonts directly from Google's servers, which may log standard request data (such as IP address) as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API - used only for the optional fun-fact feature described above.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting tools that track you across apps or sites.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, or any other personal identifiers. We do not require sign-up or login. We do not sell or share your information with data brokers or advertisers.

Children's Privacy

Zman is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because all app data is stored locally on-device and no account is required, no personal information is collected from any user, including children.

Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored data (location, alarms, preferences) remains on your device until you clear the App's storage/cache or uninstall the App, at which point it is deleted. Since we do not store this data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
